# Bank auction properties in Tamil Nadu

## Two kinds of sale

1. **SARFAESI sales by banks**: the bank has taken possession after a default and sells to recover the loan. The listing names the bank and branch.
2. **Liquidation auctions**: the seller is a named insolvency professional acting as liquidator of a company under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. Recent Tamil Nadu examples include assets of Hindustan Photo Films Mfg Co Limited in the Nilgiris and Ind-Barath Power (Madras) Limited in Tuticorin. These are often large lots, and the process documents come from the liquidator, not a bank branch.

## Check before you bid

- **Sale notice**: reserve price, EMD, inspection date, bid increment and payment timeline.
- **Title and land record**: patta, chitta and the encumbrance certificate for the survey number.
- **Occupancy**: is the property physically vacant?
- **Litigation**: search the borrower and property in court and tribunal records (DRT and NCLT).
- **Dues**: property tax, water, electricity and association charges usually pass to the buyer on an "as is where is" sale.
- **Watch the dates**: some listings carry auction dates far in the future or are re-listed after failed auctions. Confirm the date with the bank before paying an EMD.
