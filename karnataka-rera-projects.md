# Karnataka RERA projects: reading the register

India Property Register lists **9,021 projects registered with the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority**, newest registrations first. If you are buying a flat or plot in Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mangaluru or anywhere in the state, the register is the first thing to check.

## Anatomy of a Karnataka RERA number

Karnataka project numbers look like this:

`PRM/KA/RERA/1251/308/PR/091026/009008`

- **PRM/KA/RERA**: a project registered with Karnataka RERA
- **1251/308**: location codes (district and taluk). In recent registrations, 1251/308 appears on projects in **Anekal**, 1251/446 in **Bengaluru East** and 1250/304 in **Hosakote**
- **PR**: project
- **091026**: the registration date in DDMMYY form (here 9 October 2026)
- **009008**: a running serial number

If a builder's brochure gives a number that does not follow this pattern, or the project does not appear in the register, ask why before paying anything.

## What each entry tells you

- **Project and promoter name**: check that the promoter on the register is the company you are signing with.
- **Status**: for example APPROVED.
- **Project type**: Residential/Group Housing, Plotted Development or Mixed Development.
- **Declared completion date**: the date the promoter committed to. Recent registrations show completion dates from 2027 to 2033.

## Where new projects are being registered

Recent registrations cluster around Bengaluru's edges, in **Anekal, Hosakote, Bengaluru East and Yelahanka**, with a steady stream of plotted layouts in **Tumakuru**, **Dakshina Kannada/Mangaluru**, Belagavi, Koppal, Chikkaballapur and Mysuru.

## Before you book

1. Find the project on the register and match the number, promoter and location.
2. Compare the declared completion date with the date in your agreement.
3. Check the promoter's other projects for delays.
4. Search court records for the promoter's name.
5. Check the land record for the survey number.
