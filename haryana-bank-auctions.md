# Bank auction properties in Haryana: a buyer's guide

Haryana is the busiest state for bank auctions on India Property Register: **25,218 listings**, more than Maharashtra (15,682) or Uttar Pradesh (10,944) as of October 2026. That volume covers everything from a 50 sq yard house in Faridabad with a reserve under Rs 20 lakh to industrial plots with factory buildings in Panipat reserved at several crore.

## Where the listings come from

Most are **SARFAESI sales**: a bank has taken possession after a borrower defaulted and sells the property by public auction to recover the loan. Each listing shows the bank and branch, the reserve price, the location, the PIN code and the auction date. In a recent page of upcoming Haryana auctions, Canara Bank and Punjab National Bank were the most frequent sellers, with Indian Bank, Bank of India, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India and State Bank of India also listing.

Some listings are **recovery sales by a Debts Recovery Tribunal** recovery officer, titled as a "Sale Proclamation Notice in R.C. No. …". These follow tribunal procedure rather than a bank's own SARFAESI notice, so read the proclamation itself for terms.

## Reading a listing

- **Property type and size**: houses, plots, shops, industrial plots and offices, in sq yard, sq feet or sq metre. Convert to one unit before comparing.
- **Reserve price**: the minimum bid, not the market value. Compare it with the local circle rate and recent sales.
- **Auction date and status**: "Upcoming" listings are still open for inspection and EMD.
- **Branch and district**: Faridabad, Palwal, Karnal, Yamunanagar, Jind, Hisar, Sirsa, Kaithal, Fatehabad, Panipat and Panchkula all appear regularly.

## The "as is where is" warning

Banks sell on an **as is where is, as is what is** basis, so the buyer carries the risk of unpaid dues, encumbrances and litigation. Before bidding:

1. **Read the full sale notice**: reserve price, earnest money deposit (EMD), bid increment, inspection date and payment schedule.
2. **Inspect the property** on the inspection date. Check whether it is vacant or occupied.
3. **Check the title chain and land record**: Jamabandi and mutation entries for the khasra/khewat in Haryana.
4. **Search court cases** against the borrower and the property, including DRT matters.
5. **Ask about dues**: property tax, electricity, society or HSVP/HUDA charges.
6. **Budget beyond the bid**: stamp duty and registration on the sale certificate.
